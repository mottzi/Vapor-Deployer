# Deployer logo on the panel

The panel now sports the new Deployer logo (a phoenix hatching from a cracked egg) tucked into the top-left corner of the workspace, peeking out from behind the runtime header.

**What's new:**

- **Logo above the runtime header.** The Deployer logo sits in the top-left, with its bottom edge tucked ~22 px behind the header panel so it looks like it's peeking out rather than balancing on top.
- **Mobile-friendly sizing.** On narrow screens (≤480 px) the logo scales down to 80% of its desktop size and the page's top padding is bumped so the logo never gets clipped at the top of the viewport.
- **No layout side-effects.** The logo is absolutely positioned, so the sign-out button keeps its natural shape and the rest of the header layout is unchanged.
- **Login page redesigned around the logo.** The "Deployer" wordmark above the sign-in card is gone; the new logo now peeks out from behind the top edge of the card, centered horizontally, and remains a link to the project repository.
